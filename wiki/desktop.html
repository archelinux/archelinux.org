SwayFX and Litho Dark

The selected desktop adapts okubax/dotfiles's Litho Dark palette and inset Waybar panel with a centered clock. Provenance and the upstream license are installed in /usr/share/doc/arche-tui-stack/. Tiled and floating windows have a one-pixel outline. The recommended SwayFX compositor adds backdrop blur and gives Waybar and windows a nine-pixel corner radius. The workspace highlight sits eight pixels inside the panel and has a three-pixel radius. Applications that draw their own internal chrome remain responsible for that chrome.

Super+Return opens Foot; Super+d opens Fuzzel; Super+Shift+q closes a window; Super+h/j/k/l moves focus; Super+r enters resize mode; Super+Shift+c reloads configuration; Super+Shift+e exits Sway. No upstream dotfile installer or personal cloud/service configuration is run. Media keys use WirePlumber and playerctl.

Login hooks exist for Zsh, Bash and Fish. They require a local non-root TTY1 session, no existing display, and no SSH connection. NVIDIA/nouveau detection sets WLR_NO_HARDWARE_CURSORS=1 and passes --unsupported-gpu; this is a fallback, not a guarantee for every driver. No hook is added to the live root installer. useradd -m -U -k /usr/share/arche/skel copies all templates directly into the new user's home and assigns their ownership. Package templates never claim Bash-owned /etc/skel files.

Sway fills the output with Twin Blades from /usr/share/arche/twin-blades.jpg. The artwork comes from the dharmx/walls collection. Artist and redistribution license are unverified.

For automated UEFI installation acceptance on a disposable qcow2 disk, see QEMU installation tests.
