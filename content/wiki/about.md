# About archelinux

archelinux is a rolling-release Arch-based workstation with a lightweight tiling desktop. Boot the USB, choose your tools, and log in to a SwayFX desktop with Zsh, tmux and Neovim. You can edit the configuration files in your home directory.

[Download archelinux](../download/index.html) · [Install guide](installation.html) · [System architecture](system.html)

See [Use and customize the Sway desktop](desktop.html) for the workspace and its theme. Check the [hardware matrix](hardware.html) for the configurations exercised in testing.
